**Effective: May 2, 2026**

clawdemy.org sets one functional cookie, only when you choose to use the optional star feature. We set no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind.

## The single cookie we may set

| Name | Purpose | Lifetime | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cw_star_<slug>` | Records that this browser has already starred a particular lesson, so we don't double-count if you click again. One cookie per starred lesson. | 1 year | Functional |

This cookie is set by clawdemy.org on your behalf only when you click a star button on a lesson page. It contains the value `1` and identifies nothing about you, your IP, or your session. We never use it for analytics, profiling, or advertising. Under ePrivacy and GDPR, cookies that exist solely to deliver a service the user explicitly requested are exempt from the consent requirement, which is why this site has no consent banner: there is nothing to consent to until you click a star button, and the act of clicking is itself the consent.

If you never click a star button, this cookie is never set.

## How to verify in your browser

1. Open developer tools (right-click → Inspect, or Cmd+Option+I on Mac, F12 on Windows)
2. Go to the "Application" tab in Chrome or Edge, or "Storage" in Firefox or Safari
3. Click "Cookies" in the left sidebar and select clawdemy.org
4. The list will be empty unless you have starred at least one lesson, in which case you will see one `cw_star_<slug>` entry per lesson you have starred

## About browser localStorage and sessionStorage

Clawdemy uses browser storage for a few small comfort features:

- **`localStorage`** for your theme choice (light / dark / auto) and your local list of starred lessons
- **`sessionStorage`** for which sidebar sections you have expanded (cleared when you close the tab)

None of this is a cookie. None of it is sent to our servers. None of it is used to identify or track you. It exists only to make the site behave the way you set it on your next visit.

You can clear all of it by clicking "Local Storage" or "Session Storage" → clawdemy.org in the same Application tab and deleting the entries, or by using your browser's "clear site data" feature.

## Why this matters

Most websites set cookies for analytics, advertising, session management, A/B testing, or third-party integrations. We do none of those things on Clawdemy. We do not run Google Analytics. We do not use Cloudflare Web Analytics. We do not load Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Hotjar, FullStory, or any other behavioral tracking script. We do not have a login system. We do not run advertising.

The single `cw_star_<slug>` functional cookie exists only to make a feature you explicitly opted into work the way it is supposed to.

## No cookie banner

Because we set no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and because the lone functional cookie we do set is exempt from the consent requirement (it serves a service you explicitly requested by clicking the star), this site has no GDPR, CCPA, or ePrivacy obligation to display a cookie consent banner. Sites that show cookie banners do so because they set cookies that require consent. We don't, so we don't.

## Third-party services

Clawdemy is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may temporarily process request data (IP address, user agent) to deliver the site to your browser and protect against abuse. This is operational and does not result in cookies being set on this domain. Cloudflare's privacy practices are documented at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

If you embed YouTube videos or other third-party media in a lesson page in the future, those embeds may set cookies under their own domains. We do not currently embed third-party media; if that ever changes, this policy will be updated and disclosed inline on the affected lesson pages.
