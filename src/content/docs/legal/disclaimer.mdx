## Educational content

The lessons on Clawdemy are provided for general educational purposes. They are written to help readers understand how modern AI systems work and how to use them practically. They are not professional advice (technical, legal, financial, medical, or otherwise). Decisions you make about your career, your business, your code, or your life should not rest on a Clawdemy lesson alone.

## AI moves fast

The field of AI is changing quickly. Specific tools, model names, prices, capabilities, and best practices may shift between when a lesson is written and when you read it. We update lessons when changes are large enough to matter. We do not guarantee that every detail is current at the moment you read it. When something looks out of date, the GitHub repo at github.com/clawless-io/clawdemy is the place to flag it.

## AI narration

Lesson audio is generated by AI text-to-speech, not by a human voice actor. We disclose this so you can decide how much to weight tone, pacing, or pronunciation when you listen. Text remains the source of truth.
