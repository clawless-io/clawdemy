**Effective: May 2, 2026**

This is the privacy policy for clawdemy.org, the free AI literacy site published by RBJ Global LLC.

## What we collect from this site

Almost nothing personal. Clawdemy does not require an account, does not run analytics, does not have email signup forms, newsletters, contact forms, or comment systems. We do not log IP addresses beyond what Cloudflare requires to serve the site to your browser, and we do not access those logs for marketing or analytics purposes.

The single exception is the optional star feature, described below.

## The star feature

Each lesson page has an optional star button you can click to signal that the lesson was useful. If you use it, two things happen:

- We set a small first-party functional cookie on your browser named `cw_star_<slug>` (where `<slug>` identifies the lesson) so the same browser does not double-count by clicking again. The cookie holds the literal value `1` and lives for one year. It contains no identifier, no profile data, and no cross-lesson tracking. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies/) for the full breakdown.
- We increment a per-lesson aggregate count stored on our server. The count is the total number of stars that lesson has received across all visitors. It is not joined to any identity, IP, or session. We can see "lesson X has been starred 47 times"; we cannot see who starred it.

If you never click a star button, neither of those things happens.

## What stays in your browser

A few small pieces of state are stored locally so the site behaves the way you set it on your next visit. None of this is sent to advertisers, brokers, or third parties. We do not read it server-side to build a profile of you.

- **Theme preference** (light / dark / auto) in `localStorage`. Stays in your browser, never transmitted.
- **Local star UI state** in `localStorage` so the "Starred" badge stays visible without a roundtrip to the server.
- **Sidebar state** (which sections you have expanded) in `sessionStorage`, cleared when you close the tab.

`localStorage` and `sessionStorage` are not cookies. Cookies are sent to our servers automatically with every request; browser-storage values stay in your browser and are never transmitted. We mention them here for full transparency even though it is not technically required.

You can clear all of this at any time from your browser's "clear site data" feature.

## Verification

You can verify these claims yourself. Open your browser's developer tools, navigate to the Network tab, and reload any page on this site. You will see requests for the page itself, fonts, and stylesheets. You will not see Google Analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking script. Open the Application tab and look at Cookies for clawdemy.org. The list will be empty unless you have starred at least one lesson, in which case you will see one `cw_star_<slug>` entry per lesson you have starred. Look at Local Storage and Session Storage and you will see only the keys described above.

## Search

Clawdemy uses Pagefind, a client-side search engine. When you search the site, the index is downloaded to your browser and the search runs entirely on your device. Your search queries are never sent to a server.

## Audio lessons (podcast feed)

If you subscribe to the Clawdemy podcast feed, your podcast app downloads audio files directly from clawdemy.org (or from the CDN serving them). We do not run podcast-host analytics that match listens to listeners. Aggregate request counts at the CDN level exist for operational purposes (capacity planning, abuse prevention) and are not joined to any identity.

## GitHub

Clawdemy's curriculum is open source on GitHub at github.com/clawless-io/clawdemy. If you star or fork the repo, that interaction is governed by GitHub's privacy policy, not ours.

## Children

Clawdemy is designed for people who want to understand AI, including students and younger learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because there is no account, no form, and no comment system, there is no mechanism through which a child's information could reach us. If you are a parent or guardian with questions, contact us at info@rbjglobal.com.

## Parent company

Clawdemy is published by RBJ Global LLC. The umbrella privacy policy at rbjglobal.com/legal/privacy applies to the parent company; this policy applies to your interactions with clawdemy.org specifically.

## Changes

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Effective" date at the top and post the change. Because this site collects almost no personal data, changes will be rare.
