**Last updated: May 11, 2026.**

**Clawdemy is built to collect almost nothing about you.** No accounts, no analytics, no email signup, no tracking pixels, no ad networks. The page loads, you read or listen, and nothing of you is captured along the way. The one small functional exception is described below.

## No accounts

There is no signup form, no login, no email field anywhere on the site. Clawdemy does not maintain an account system. Reading is anonymous by design.

## No tracking, no analytics

We do not run Google Analytics, Plausible, PostHog, Mixpanel, Fathom, or any other analytics service. We have not embedded ad networks, Facebook pixels, or third-party measurement scripts. We do not set tracking cookies. You can confirm this for yourself by opening your browser's developer tools, loading any page on the site, and watching the Network and Cookies panels.

## CDN traffic for delivery only

Clawdemy is served through Cloudflare. Cloudflare logs request metadata, including IP addresses, for the operational purpose of routing traffic and defending against abuse. Retention follows Cloudflare's standard policy. We do not run logging or analytics on top of Cloudflare's records, and we do not access Cloudflare's request data for marketing, audience segmentation, or profiling.

## What is stored locally, and why

A few small pieces of state live inside your browser so the site behaves the way you set it on your next visit. None of this is transmitted to us, except where noted:

- **Theme preference** in `localStorage`.
- **Sidebar state** in `sessionStorage`, cleared when you close the tab.
- **Star-feature state** in `localStorage`, only if you have clicked a lesson's star button.

If you click a star to mark a lesson useful, a small first-party functional cookie named `cw_star_<slug>` is set so the same browser does not double-count, and a per-lesson aggregate count increments on the server. The aggregate is "lesson X has been starred N times," not joined to identity, IP, or session. If you never click a star, no cookies are set at all.

Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/) and [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies/).

## What we commit to never doing

- No email lists, newsletters, or "subscribe to updates" forms.
- No waitlists.
- No follow-on-X promotions, social re-targeting, or social-network analytics.
- No read-tracking or "you've read N percent of this lesson" beaconing.
- No selling, sharing, or licensing of visitor data, because there is no visitor data to sell.

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and the Privacy Policy first, before any change ships.
