Each track is a sequenced set of lessons aimed at a specific audience. Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Track 1: Getting Started with Clawless

For readers brand-new to Clawless. Install, first chat, first agent, all in 30 minutes. The fastest path to your first "I built something" moment.

*Status: first lesson live; more in progress.*

## Track 2: Use Case Cookbook

"I want to do X" recipes for research, email triage, code review, meeting notes, travel planning, and more. Copy-pasteable, runnable in Clawless.

*Status: Phase 3.*

## Track 3: Agent Building 101

Skills, tools, channels, cron. The extension layer of Clawless, explained without jargon.

*Status: Phase 6+.*

## Track 4: OpenClaw Deep Dive

For developers and power users. Engine internals, MCP, custom providers. Goes where the product docs don't.

*Status: Phase 6+.*

## Track 5: AI Foundations

Transformers, attention, agents, RAG, tool use. Platform-neutral at the conceptual level, for readers who want to understand how it works, not just use it.

Practice exercises in this track occasionally use trigonometry, 2D rotation matrices, and softmax by hand. A calculator and comfortable algebra and trig will help, but the worked solutions show every step if you'd rather read than compute.

*Status: full track live, adapted from Stanford CME 295 (Lectures 1 through 9), organized into seven phases.*

## Track 6: Privacy & Local-First AI

Data flows, vendor-policy reading, threat models, local-first architecture, rights exercise, and personal privacy plans. For the privacy-conscious reader who wants to use AI without trading away her data, her clients' data, or her family's data to do it.

*Status: Phase 1 live (2 lessons); more in progress.*

## Track 7: Pain Point Library

One article per recurring question from the AI community. Auto-grown from the agentic content pipeline; human-approved before publish.

*Status: Phase 6+.*
