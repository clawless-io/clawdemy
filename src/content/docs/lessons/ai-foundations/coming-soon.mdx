This lecture is on the roadmap for our adaptation of Stanford CME 295: Transformers & Large Language Models, but is not yet published as Clawdemy lessons. The Stanford lecture itself is freely available on the [course YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOCXd21gf0CF4xr35yINeOy), and the [Amidi twins' super-cheatsheet](https://cme295.stanford.edu/cheatsheet/) (MIT-licensed) is the best single-page reference for the topics in question.

## What's currently published

The Track 5 sidebar shows every Stanford lecture in order. Lectures with published Clawdemy lessons are expandable; lectures marked **Coming soon** are placeholders that link here.

To track progress, the [tracks index](/tracks/) lists every published lesson in publication order.

## How to follow Stanford ahead of us

If you want to keep moving through the Stanford material before we have adapted it:

1. Watch the lecture on the [Stanford CME 295 YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOCXd21gf0CF4xr35yINeOy).
2. Read the relevant section of the [Amidi cheatsheet](https://cme295.stanford.edu/cheatsheet/) for the dense single-page version.
3. Come back when the Clawdemy adaptation is published.

The full Stanford CME 295 course site is at [cme295.stanford.edu](https://cme295.stanford.edu/).

## Why we publish in lecture-by-lecture batches

Each Stanford lecture is treated as a complete unit. We publish one lecture's worth of lessons at a time, then pause for review before opening the next lecture. This keeps the structure mapped clearly to the Stanford course and gives readers natural revision points.
