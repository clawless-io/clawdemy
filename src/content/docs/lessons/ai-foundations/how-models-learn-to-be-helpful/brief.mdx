## What you'll learn

This is the seventh lesson in our adaptation of Stanford CME 295: Transformers & Large Language Models. The first six lessons covered the transformer architecture and how it generates text. What they described is a *base model*: the raw output of pretraining, a transformer that has read a lot of text and gotten good at predicting the next token. A base model is not yet a chat assistant. The thing you actually talk to has been through two more training stages after pretraining, and those stages are why it feels helpful instead of just plausible. This lesson walks through both of them: supervised fine-tuning, where the model learns to follow instructions, and reinforcement learning from human feedback, where it learns which kinds of responses people rated higher in training. Course materials are at [cme295.stanford.edu](https://cme295.stanford.edu/); this lesson draws on the later sections of the syllabus that cover post-pretraining adaptation.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** Lessons 1 and 6 are strongly recommended. Lesson 1 (attention) is the mechanism the assistant builds on; Lesson 6 (text generation) explains what "the model produces tokens one at a time" actually means in operation. We assume both are familiar. If "logits," "softmax," or "next-token prediction" feel unfamiliar, read those lessons first.

## By the end, you'll be able to

- Explain the three-stage training pipeline (pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, RLHF) and what each stage adds to a base model
- Describe what supervised fine-tuning does and why instruction-following emerges from it
- Walk through the RLHF loop in plain language (reward model, policy optimization, the human-feedback signal)
- Identify which observable behaviors of modern AI assistants come from pretraining versus fine-tuning versus RLHF

## Time and difficulty

- **Read time:** about 25 minutes
- **Practice time:** about 15 minutes (a comparison exercise: identify which stage produced which observed behavior on real assistant outputs)
- **Difficulty:** standard
