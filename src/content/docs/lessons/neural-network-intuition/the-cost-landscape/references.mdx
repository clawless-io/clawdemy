## Watch this next

- **[Gradient descent, how neural networks learn (3Blue1Brown)](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/gradient-descent)** by Grant Sanderson. The chapter this lesson mirrors. The middle section animates exactly the picture built here: a ball on a 2D cost surface, the slope at its feet, and the downhill direction. Seeing the surface tilt and the ball find a valley is the fastest way to lock in the landscape intuition.

## Going deeper

A short, durable list. Each link is a specific next step, not a generic pile.

- **[The Essence of Calculus (3Blue1Brown)](https://www.3blue1brown.com/?topic=calculus)** by Grant Sanderson, and Clawdemy's own **Track 8 (Visual Math: Calculus)**. This lesson leaned on "the slope at a point" without defining it precisely. If that idea felt shaky, the derivative is exactly what makes it precise, and both of these build it from scratch.

- **[Neural Networks and Deep Learning, Chapter 1 (gradient descent section)](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/chap1.html)** by Michael Nielsen. Introduces the same valley-and-ball picture and then connects it to the formula for the gradient. The natural deeper read once you want the math under the metaphor.

- **[TensorFlow Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org/)**. Press play and watch the loss fall step by step. You are watching a walk downhill across a cost landscape exactly like the one in this lesson, one small step at a time.
