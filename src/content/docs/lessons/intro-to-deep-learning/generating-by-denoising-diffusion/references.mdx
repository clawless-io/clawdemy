## Going deeper

A short, durable list. Each link is a specific next step, not a generic pile.

- **["Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" (Ho, Jain, and Abbeel, 2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239)**. The paper that made diffusion practical and kicked off the modern wave of diffusion image generators. The primary source for this lesson; technical, but the forward-noising and reverse-denoising structure described here is exactly its backbone.

- **[What are Diffusion Models? (Lilian Weng)](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2021-07-11-diffusion-models/)**. A widely recommended written walk-through that bridges the intuition here to the actual math, the forward process, the reverse process, training, and conditioning, in careful steps. The best next read once "remove a little noise, many times" feels solid and you want the equations.
