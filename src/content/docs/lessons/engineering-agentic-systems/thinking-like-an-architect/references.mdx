## Source material

This lesson's workflow and agent definitions, and its simplicity principle, are quoted verbatim from Anthropic's public engineering essay. Everything else in the lesson is Clawdemy's own synthesis and examples.

- **[Building Effective AI Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)**, Anthropic engineering blog, December 19, 2024. The essay behind this lesson's central distinction. It defines workflows ("systems where LLMs and tools are orchestrated through predefined code paths") and agents ("systems where LLMs dynamically direct their own processes and tool usage, maintaining control over how they accomplish tasks"), catalogs the common workflow patterns, and closes with the principle this track treats as law: "Success in the LLM space isn't about building the most sophisticated system. It's about building the right system for your needs."
