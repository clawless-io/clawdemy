## Going deeper inside Clawless Computer

Two follow-on KB chapters that touch what CostGuard and the privacy posture do not cover directly.

- **Clawless Computer Knowledge Base, "Usage and Cost" chapter.** The Usage dashboard in detail, including the per-provider, per-model, per-conversation breakdown that the lesson asks you to open. Useful when you want to understand where a specific dollar amount on your provider invoice came from.

- **Clawless Computer Knowledge Base, "Chat and Models" chapter.** The model picker and the cost-per-model framing. Picking smaller models for routine work is the largest single lever on your BYOK spend, and that chapter is where to look when you want to make that decision deliberately.

## Going deeper outside Clawless Computer

Three outside sources for readers who want context on local-first design, AI provider data policies, and personal AI spending discipline.

- **["Local-first software"](https://www.inkandswitch.com/local-first/)** (Ink and Switch, 2019). The original essay defining the local-first design principles that the Clawless Computer privacy posture follows. Reads as a manifesto, but the seven principles in section 3 are a useful checklist for evaluating any tool that claims to be local-first. The "your data stays on your computer" framing in this lesson sits inside the broader local-first lineage this essay defines.

- **[Anthropic privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)** and **[OpenAI privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)**. Two of the major providers Clawless Computer can route through. Read both at least once if any of your messages contain anything you would not want a model trained on. Both default to not training on API customer data, but the wording matters and the policies evolve, so the read-once-then-revisit cadence is the right one.

- **[The Verge: "AI assistants are getting expensive"](https://www.theverge.com/24102873/ai-chatbot-cost-subscription-personal-use)** (March 2024, illustrative). A general-press piece on what personal AI usage actually costs. Not Clawless Computer specific. Useful for calibration if you are wondering whether the dollar amounts you see in your Usage dashboard are normal, high, or low compared to what other people running AI at home are spending.
