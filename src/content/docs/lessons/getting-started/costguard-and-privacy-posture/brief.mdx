## What you'll learn

This is the lesson that answers the two questions almost everyone has on their first week with [Clawless Computer](https://clawless.ai). The first is "what if I accidentally rack up a giant bill?" The answer is **CostGuard**: a software safety net you configure once with a monthly cap, a warn threshold, and a hard-stop behavior. Pay-as-you-go API calls count toward the cap; local models do not. With Block on (the default), the cap can stop what Clawless Computer itself starts, while channel replies and scheduled jobs count toward your total but cannot be stopped while they run. An amber banner on the home screen marks the warn threshold, and a red banner marks the cap. The second question is "who is actually seeing my conversations?" The answer is short. The data path is your computer, the AI provider you picked, your computer. There is no Clawless Computer server holding your conversations, and there is no license server or account. The lesson also covers what survives if the company behind Clawless Computer ever shut down (your data; it is on your computer) and the four-party trust model worth applying to any tool, skill, or integration you install.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** lesson 3, *Connecting your AI provider with an API key*, where you connected at least one AI provider. You need Clawless Computer installed with that provider connected; ideally you have run a few real conversations so the Usage dashboard has actual numbers to look at. The practice is a 20-minute setup (configure CostGuard, open the Usage dashboard, find your data folder and the About section).

## By the end, you'll be able to

- Configure a CostGuard cap, warn threshold, and hard-stop behavior in Settings, Budget, and predict how the warnings will signal you (an amber banner on the home screen at the warn threshold, a red banner at the cap)
- Distinguish what counts toward the cap (pay-as-you-go API keys) from what does not (local models, models with no known price, image generation), and what the cap can stop from what it only counts
- Trace the data path of a single message from your keyboard to the agent's reply, and identify the one server in the path
- Identify the two places your stuff lives (your computer and the AI provider) and what each one holds
- Apply the four-party trust model to evaluate any new tool, skill, or integration before installing it

## Time and difficulty

- **Read time:** about twenty minutes
- **Practice time:** about 20 minutes (set a monthly cap that feels comfortable, pick warn and hard-stop, save; open the Usage dashboard for the past week; find your data folder and the About section)
