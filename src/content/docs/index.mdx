Lessons are narrated and developed right here in Dallas under the banner of RBJ Global LLC.</p>

		<p>No. Clawdemy is written for the worried generalist, not the curious developer. We start from the foundations and build up, one step at a time.</p>
	</details>
	<details>
		<summary>How is this different from just asking ChatGPT?</summary>
		<p>A chatbot answers the question you already know to ask. Clawdemy builds understanding in order, so you learn what to ask, and how to tell whether the answer is right.</p>
	</details>
	<details>
		<summary>Do you track me?</summary>
		<p>No. The site runs one privacy-first, cookieless visit counter that counts page views only, sets no cookies, and identifies no one. It is open source, so you can audit it yourself.</p>
	</details>

		<summary>Is there an official Claude certification?</summary>
		<p>Yes. Anthropic runs an official certification program for people who build with Claude. We wrote a plain-English explainer on what it is, what it costs, and who can take it today: <a href="/claude-certification/">Claude Certification (CCA-F), explained</a>.</p>
	</details>
	<details>
		<summary>Is the audio AI-generated?</summary>
