## What Clawdemy is

Clawdemy is free, public AI education for the people who are most worried that AI will replace them. Articles, walkthroughs, and practical guides for turning fear into fluency.

We believe anyone can become genuinely powerful with AI. Not a passive user of someone else's assistant, but someone who composes, automates, and owns their own workflows.

## What's live today

Seven lessons are live in **Track 5: AI Foundations**. Our adaptation of [Stanford CME 295](https://cme295.stanford.edu/) takes you from how AI reads text (tokenization, why "strawberry" trips up some modern AI models) through how a transformer generates one token at a time, and on to how a base model becomes the chat assistant you actually talk to (supervised fine-tuning, RLHF). Hand-drawn diagrams, hands-on practice exercises, click-to-reveal flashcards, and an AI-narrated audio version on every lesson.
