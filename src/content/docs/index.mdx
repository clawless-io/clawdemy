A free 24-track AI curriculum, spanning foundational math through frontier AI. Hand-written, narrated, built lesson by lesson. No signup, no paywall, no ads.

Lessons are built on the foundations taught in open courses from MIT, Stanford, Berkeley, Hugging Face, and Anthropic Academy.

**New here? Follow a learning path.** Pick a path below, like *Foundations*, and we will guide you through it track by track. Every lesson links to the next track in your path, so you always know where to go next. Prefer to explore? Browse any track and start anywhere.
